# Decorative Blocks — Build Authentic Medieval Taverns

Decorative Blocks 9minecraft Mod adds 16 new building parts to your game. It gives you everything you need to make old-style medieval taverns and rustic houses. You can use these pieces to make your wooden cabins or stone castles look much better.

Thatch Roofs: Adds golden straw tiles that are perfect for farmhouses and old huts. Stone Lattices: Gives you transparent stone mesh windows to let light in while keeping mobs out.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Big Energy**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
